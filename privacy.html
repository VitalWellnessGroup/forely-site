Privacy Policy

Forely for iPhone · Effective October 31, 2026

HomeSupport
The short version. Forely has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your calendar data. Your events and reminders stay in Apple Calendar and Reminders on your device. Your data leaves the device in three cases only, all optional or handled by Apple: an AI provider you choose yourself, Apple's weather service, and your own iCloud.

This policy explains how Forely ("the app"), published by Vital Wellness Group LLC ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Forely iOS app and its widgets, Live Activities, Siri and Shortcuts actions.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information. We have no user accounts and no access to your calendars, reminders, location or usage. We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

2. Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, Forely reads and writes your events and reminders through Apple's EventKit framework, on your device. Time blocks Forely creates are saved as events in a "Forely" calendar in your Apple Calendar. Edits you make in Forely are written back to Apple Calendar and Reminders. This data is not sent to us. It syncs across your devices only through Apple's own Calendar and Reminders sync, under Apple's terms.

3. Forely's own data

Forely stores a small amount of its own data on your device: links between prep tasks and events, outdoor flags, notification choices, themes and other settings, and any Ask Forely conversation you pin. If "Sync with iCloud" is on, this data is stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot read your private iCloud database. You can turn sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync.

4. AI features

Apple Intelligence (default)

By default, Forely's AI features use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. That processing is done by Apple's on-device model and is not sent to us.

Optional third-party AI providers

You can choose to connect Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or xAI (Grok) with your own API key. Forely shows a consent screen naming the provider before anything is sent. When you use an external provider, your device sends requests directly to that provider, not through us. They contain:

  • the text you type into Quick Capture or Ask Forely, with location-like text removed where Forely can detect it;
  • titles, dates and times of relevant events and tasks.

Forely never sends event notes, attendees or event locations to AI providers. Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain, on that device only, and is never sent to us. Data you send to a provider is handled under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. You can switch back to Apple Intelligence or remove your key at any time in Settings → AI.

5. Weather and location

If you turn on weather, Forely gets forecasts from Apple's WeatherKit service. To do this, Forely sends Apple a location in one of three forms:

  • your device's approximate location, if you grant permission (Forely asks for it only when you turn weather on);
  • a city you enter in Settings;
  • for an event, the address or place you enter for that event.

Addresses and place names are converted to coordinates using Apple's MapKit. Location is used only to fetch forecasts. It is not sent to us or to any AI provider. Forecasts are cached on your device. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

6. Purchases

Forely Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes all payments. We never receive your payment details. Forely checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

7. Notifications, Siri, widgets and Live Activities

Heads-ups, daily summaries and weekly briefings are local notifications scheduled on your device. Siri and Shortcuts requests are processed by Apple. Widgets and Live Activities read data from a container on your device that only Forely can access.

8. Children

Forely is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove Forely's data:

  • delete the app;
  • to remove synced data, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Forely.

Events and reminders, including the "Forely" calendar, stay in Apple Calendar and Reminders until you delete them there.

10. Security

Data stays on your device or in Apple's services, protected by iOS. API keys are kept in the Keychain. Connections to AI providers and Apple services use encrypted HTTPS.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Forely handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes, such as adding a Forely-operated AI service, will be described in the app before they take effect.
